Terms and Conditions

Please review these booking and payment terms before submitting a request.

Booking requests

A booking request is not a confirmed reservation. Program details and availability must be confirmed before travel arrangements are final.

Prices

Prices shown on the site are per person. Confirm availability, itinerary, inclusions, and the final cost before making a payment.

Deposits and cancellations

For paid trips, the deposit must be paid by the due date provided. The remaining balance is due once your program is confirmed. If you cancel after confirmation, your deposit is non-refundable.

Payments and personal information

This site does not process payments. Choosing a payment method only records a preference in the form; payment instructions must be arranged separately.

The booking form is a static demonstration and does not transmit or store submitted information. Do not enter real identity or contact details until secure form processing is configured.

These terms reflect the current information shown on this site and should be reviewed before accepting real bookings.
